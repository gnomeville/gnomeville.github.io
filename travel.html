Featured Destination No. 001 · Arts & Heritage

The Museum of Strategic Pecan History

One pecan. One legend. One very important museum.

Discover Gnomeville's newest cultural institution, established under the distinguished patronage of Raven. Marvel at the original pecan in its protected display, contemplate the First Rook–Raven Summit, and explore a vision of cultural preservation so ambitious that its endowment remains exactly 0¢.

Highlights: The Original Pecan Chamber, Raven Wing, Strategic Syrup Reserves Archive, Black Orchard café and Rook's museum gift shop.
