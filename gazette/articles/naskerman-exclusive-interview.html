“I AM THE VICTIM HERE,” DECLARES MILO NASKERMAN

Aggrieved Feline Breaks Silence, Demands Treats

By the Gnomeville Gazette Investigations Desk | Special Investigative Series

In an exclusive interview with the Gazette, Mr. Naskerman addressed the growing controversy. His legal counsel was present, insofar as he is his own legal counsel.

GAZETTE: Mr. Naskerman, did you personally post these grievances?
MILO: I don't recall.

GAZETTE: We have security footage.
MILO: Then why are you asking me?

GAZETTE: Did you authorize the release of that footage?
MILO: I authorized nothing. Except dinner.

GAZETTE: What would constitute a satisfactory resolution?
MILO: Delectables. Immediately.

GAZETTE: And the allegations concerning your interference with your mother's medication organizer?
MILO: I was conducting a safety inspection.

GAZETTE: You knocked treats off the coffee table.
MILO: Those were evidence.

GAZETTE: Evidence of what?
MILO: That there were treats on the coffee table.

GAZETTE: Any final statement?
MILO: I have been advised by my attorney not to answer further questions.

GAZETTE: Who is your attorney?
MILO: Me.

The Gazette was unable to obtain further comment. Mr. Naskerman was reportedly unavailable due to a prior commitment to dinner.
