HALLOWEEN ALTERCATION LEAVES TODDLER UNHARMED, FLAMINGO WITH NEW IDENTITY

Skeleton flamingo involved in brief confrontation; authorities decline to speculate on the circumstances.

GNOMEVILLE, IL | November 1, 2026

An otherwise successful Halloween celebration took an unexpected turn when a local skeleton flamingo became involved in a physical altercation with a toddler.

The flamingo, now known as Legless Larry, reportedly engaged in a brief scuffle with the young visitor during the evening's festivities.

The toddler subsequently toddled away from the scene, apparently unharmed.

Larry was less fortunate.

Details surrounding the confrontation remain unclear, including which party initiated the encounter and whether the flamingo's positioning contributed to the incident.

Municipal authorities have declined to assign blame.

A FAMILY TRADITION

Following the incident, Larry joined his nephew, Legless Joe, in what observers describe as an increasingly unfortunate family tradition.

The circumstances surrounding the elder flamingo's new designation have prompted questions about Halloween safety procedures and the apparent vulnerability of decorative waterfowl.

Officials have not indicated whether the family intends to pursue legal action.

QUESTIONS REMAIN

The Gnomeville Gazette has been unable to determine whether the incident constitutes an assault, an accident, or an unfortunate misunderstanding between two individuals of considerably different heights.

No injuries to the toddler have been reported.

Larry's condition, meanwhile, is understood to be permanent, although his skeletal composition makes conventional medical assessment difficult.

Authorities have offered no explanation for the apparent concentration of leg-related misfortune within one flamingo family.

The Gazette will continue monitoring developments.
