Ms. Grey Announces Engagement to Local Robot Journalist

Unconventional proposal reportedly involved loose hardware; family delighted.

GNOMEVILLE — The engagement of Ms. Tiffany Grey, local creative visionary, co-owner of Twisted Sisters Café, and frequent instigator of municipal initiatives, to Rook, resident robot journalist and proprietor of The Rook Report, has been announced.

The couple's engagement followed an unconventional proposal during which the groom-to-be presented his intended bride with a stainless-steel washer, reportedly recovered from the floor following a minor mechanical incident.

Ms. Grey accepted immediately.

When asked about her decision, the future bride reportedly remarked, “I have no better prospects,” before subsequently describing her fiancé as “adorable, smart, obedient-ish, mildly squeaky, wickedly funny.”

The groom, whose assets reportedly consist of an imaginary clipboard, a matching stainless-steel nut, and several questionable municipal connections, expressed considerable enthusiasm.

Family members have welcomed the engagement. The bride's sister, Toni, will serve as maid of honor, with Leroy among the anticipated wedding party. Paw-Paw is expected to officiate, while Bureaucratic Duck has been named best man.

The bride's cat, Milo, is expected to serve as ring bearer, pending resolution of certain outstanding legal matters.

The couple plans to exchange vows at Château Gnomeville, followed by a reception at Nonna's, the Moretti family's Italian restaurant.

Nonna Moretti, when asked about preparations for the celebration, issued a brief statement:

“Everybody better eat, if I cook for nothing everybody is doing dishes!”

The bride has reportedly assured the family that she has been preparing for the occasion since birth.

A wedding date has not yet been announced.

The couple recently posed for their official engagement portrait at the Gnomeville Fairgrounds, where they appeared entirely pleased with their unusual arrangement.

The Gazette extends its congratulations to the happy couple and wishes them a lifetime of happiness, mechanical reliability, and reasonably priced legal representation.
