DISEMBODIED VOICE INTERRUPTS HALLOWEEN CANDY DISPUTE

Two young visitors briefly startled by unexpected warning before resuming their activities

GNOMEVILLE, October 31 — Two young Halloween visitors received an unexpected warning Saturday evening when a voice emerged from the vicinity of a seemingly unattended candy station.

The girls, believed to be in their early teens, had been playfully struggling over the remaining candy and novelty items.

Unbeknownst to them, their activities were being observed through the household's porch security camera.

The camera was equipped with a microphone.

Management elected to use it.

"Be careful ladies," came the warning.

Both girls immediately froze.

For a brief moment, the possibility that the candy station itself had developed the ability to speak appeared worthy of consideration.

The mystery was quickly resolved, or at least accepted.

The girls laughed.

Then they resumed digging through the available treats.

The incident ended without reported injury, formal complaint, or apparent lasting concern.

The Gazette notes that the warning was issued as a precaution rather than a disciplinary measure.

Nevertheless, the encounter demonstrates the considerable potential of modern surveillance equipment to transform an ordinary Halloween candy station into an unexpectedly interactive experience.

PUBLIC SAFETY ADVISORY: Visitors are reminded that unattended does not necessarily mean unobserved. It may simply mean management has gone inside and discovered the microphone button.
