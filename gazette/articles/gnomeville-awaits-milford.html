GNOMEVILLE AWAITS MILFORD'S RETURN

FROM THE ARCHIVES | October 2026, before Milford's arrival | THE GNOMEVILLE GAZETTE

Beloved seasonal resident expected to emerge from annual rejuvenation.

Few annual events are anticipated with as much affection as the return of Milford, Gnomeville's beloved jack-o'-lantern.

Each autumn, Milford emerges from the earth following a period of rest and rejuvenation, returning to spend the Halloween season among his friends and neighbors.

Known for his gentle disposition, unhurried manner of speaking, and tendency to become embarrassed by excessive attention, Milford has long held a special place in the hearts of Gnomeville residents.

His appearances are not scheduled through conventional municipal channels.

He simply arrives when the time is right.

Although no official date has been announced, anticipation is growing throughout the community.

"We always look forward to seeing him," said one resident. "He's just such a lovely fellow."

At the conclusion of the season, Milford returns to the earth, where he rests until the following autumn.

For now, Gnomeville waits.
