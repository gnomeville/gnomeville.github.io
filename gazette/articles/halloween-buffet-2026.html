HALLOWEEN BUFFET SUSTAINS STAFF FOLLOWING DEMANDING DISTRIBUTION OPERATIONS

Household culinary departments coordinate substantial seasonal spread

GNOMEVILLE, November 1 — Following an evening of candy distribution, visitor supervision, and extensive physical exertion, Halloween personnel withdrew indoors to a substantial buffet prepared by the household's culinary staff.

The meal represented a coordinated effort involving Tiffany, Toni, and Billy, each of whom contributed dishes to the festivities.

Tiffany prepared taco casserole and BBQ meatballs, providing two substantial offerings for the evening's hungry personnel.

Billy contributed chili dip, a familiar addition to the household's gathering.

Toni's contributions included skeleton dogs, sausage or pepperoni pizza towers accompanied by marinara dipping sauce, and bloody eyeball devilish eggs.

The skeleton dogs were particularly appropriate to the occasion, although their name briefly raised questions regarding the welfare of Bowser, Gnomeville's resident skeletal canine.

Management has confirmed that no Bowser was harmed in the creation of the skeleton dogs.

The Gazette considers this reassurance both welcome and necessary.

The buffet was available as the household recovered from the demands of the evening, which included the distribution of nearly two bins of candy and supervision of numerous visiting trick-or-treaters.

By approximately 7:30 p.m., personnel had largely withdrawn from active distribution duties.

The transition from candy service to buffet consumption marked the unofficial conclusion of the evening's most physically demanding activities.

No formal assessment of leftovers has been submitted.

Nor has the municipality established whether the culinary department will receive hazard pay for operating simultaneously as a restaurant, confectionery warehouse, and Halloween visitor center.

FOOD DESK ADVISORY: The Gazette reminds readers that skeleton dogs are a seasonal food item and should not be confused with Bowser, who remains a resident rather than an entrée.
