PAW-PAW AND GERTIE DRAW ADMIRERS DURING HALLOWEEN APPEARANCE

Gnomeville residents become popular subjects for visitors' photographs

GNOMEVILLE, October 31 — Two of Gnomeville's most recognizable residents enjoyed considerable attention Saturday evening as Halloween visitors stopped to photograph Paw-Paw and Gertie.

The pair proved popular with children and adults alike, attracting admirers throughout the festivities.

Visitors paused to pose for pictures, transforming the Halloween display into an impromptu celebrity appearance.

The enthusiasm was particularly notable given that neither resident had announced a formal meet-and-greet, established a photography schedule, or retained a publicist.

The lack of administrative preparation did not appear to diminish public interest.

Paw-Paw, who also serves as a judge in Gnomeville, has not issued a statement concerning his growing public profile.

Gertie's response has likewise not been formally recorded.

The Gazette understands that the appearances occurred amid an already demanding evening of candy distribution, visitor management, and general Halloween activity.

Their popularity nevertheless provided a welcome reminder that Gnomeville's residents continue to attract attention beyond the municipality's immediate population.

Whether future appearances will require designated photography areas, crowd-control measures, or commemorative merchandise remains to be determined.

For now, the photographs themselves will serve as evidence of a successful public engagement.

SOCIETY DESK: No admission fee was charged, and no autograph policy was announced. The Gazette considers both omissions administratively troubling.
