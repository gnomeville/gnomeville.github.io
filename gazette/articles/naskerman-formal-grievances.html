LOCAL FELINE FILES THREE FORMAL GRIEVANCES AGAINST MANAGEMENT

Treat Shortages, Unauthorized Hissing, and Bureaucratic Negligence Alleged

By the Gnomeville Gazette Investigations Desk | Special Investigative Series

In a stunning escalation of household tensions, Milo Arthur Lestat Naskerman has accused Management of widespread administrative misconduct.

Management has yet to respond. Sources close to the investigation suggest Management may not even be aware that it has been sued.

The complainant has demanded immediate redress, preferably in a squeezable pouch.
