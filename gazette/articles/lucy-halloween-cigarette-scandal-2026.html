LUCY REFUSES TO APOLOGIZE!

HALLOWEEN CIGARETTE SCANDAL ROCKS GNOMEVILLE | Local celebrity attracts admirers, criticism, and one spectacularly unhelpful management statement

GNOMEVILLE, October 31 — Lucy, Gnomeville's notoriously unapologetic goose, found herself at the center of controversy Saturday evening after a Halloween visitor expressed disapproval of the cigarette in her mouth.

The incident occurred during the municipality's Halloween festivities, where Lucy was attracting considerable attention from visitors.

While many appeared delighted by the local celebrity's presence, at least one woman was scandalized by her smoking habit.

Management was confronted with the matter.

The response was immediate.

"What can I say, she's a mean old goose."

The statement did little to suggest that Lucy would be changing her behavior.

Nor did it indicate that management intended to pursue disciplinary action, smoking cessation counseling, or a formal review of the goose's public image.

For longtime observers of Lucy's career, the controversy is unlikely to come as a surprise.

The celebrity has cultivated a reputation for maintaining her own standards of conduct, regardless of public expectations.

Her appearance during Halloween festivities offered yet another opportunity for admirers and critics alike to encounter her distinctive personality.

Whether the cigarette will affect future public appearances remains unknown.

Lucy herself has not provided a verified statement concerning the incident.

GMZ has therefore declined to attribute any direct quotations to her.

Nevertheless, the controversy has raised an important question:

Can a goose truly be canceled if she never cared what anyone thought in the first place?

At press time, management's description of Lucy as a mean old goose remained the only confirmed public response.
