LF-101

STILL MISSING: MUNICIPAL MASTER SKELETON KEY

SECOND AND INCREASINGLY URGENT NOTICE FROM THE GNOMEVILLE POLICE DEPARTMENT. The municipal master skeleton key has NOT been recovered. It may open several municipal facilities, including rooms the Department continues to deny exist. Officers request that anyone possessing a suspiciously versatile old key return it immediately, discreetly and preferably before anyone notices the doors. PLEASE. DO NOT TEST IT. DO NOT DUPLICATE IT. DO NOT TAKE IT TO THE LIBRARY. A reward is being discussed with the Budget Office, which has now stopped answering calls. The Department reiterates that there is absolutely no reason to panic. Anyone with information is asked to contact the Police Department directly. This notice supersedes no previous denials.
