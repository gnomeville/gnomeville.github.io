From the Editorial Desk · Our Position

The Squirrels Are Not Above the Law. They Are Merely Above the Porch.

The Editorial Board | October 2026

For too long, Gnomeville has permitted a well-organized faction of squirrels to operate under the protection of their own attractiveness. Residents have documented porch damage, suspicious loitering, and attacks on innocent flamingo infrastructure. In response, the squirrels have offered neither apology nor a coherent public statement.

The Gazette recognizes the importance of due process. We also recognize the importance of not having one's solar panel carried away by a rodent.

Captain Nutters and his associates are entitled to the presumption of innocence. They are not entitled to the birdseed.

THE EDITORIAL BOARD

Guest Opinion · The Honorable Paw-Paw

Order in the Court Begins With Order in the Neighborhood

By Judge Paw-Paw | Special Contribution

As a sitting judge, I am frequently asked to weigh matters of great civic importance. I have therefore prepared the following guidance for residents who appear to have confused a neighborhood disagreement with a constitutional crisis.

Not every offense is actionable. An unpleasant glance is not assault. Someone taking the last pastry is not automatically theft. And while the repeated relocation of a garden ornament may suggest criminal intent, the Court requires evidence beyond the fact that somebody is “acting squirrelly.”

I further remind citizens that my courtroom is not an appropriate venue for settling disputes over lawn decorations, coffee strength, or who was supposed to take out the rubbish. These matters belong in mediation, preferably somewhere I cannot hear them.

Finally, parties are advised that banging a gavel is a judicial function, not an invitation for everyone else to start shouting.

Order will be maintained. I have no reason to believe this, but it remains the official position of the Court.

Hon. Paw-Paw
Presiding Judge, Gnomeville

The views expressed by Judge Paw-Paw are his own and do not constitute legal advice, a binding ruling, or permission to approach the bench with snacks.

Unpopular Opinion · Community Affairs

The Gazebo Has Done Nothing Wrong

By A Concerned Structure Enthusiast

The gazebo has been blamed for nearly everything short of the weather. Yet not one witness has produced credible evidence that the gazebo has ever left its designated location. Before the next public hearing, perhaps we should ask whether the real problem is the people who insist on holding public hearings about a gazebo.
