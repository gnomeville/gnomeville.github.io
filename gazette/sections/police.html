Dispatches from Concerned Residents

Community intelligence • Squirrel activity

Residents Urged to Remain Vigilant as Squirrel Network Expands Operations

Captain Nutters and associates remain the subject of sustained neighborhood scrutiny following reports of unauthorized porch inspections, suspected structural nibbling, and the forcible relocation of equipment belonging to a solar-powered flamingo.

One witness described the organization as “a cute blight,” adding that cuteness should not be mistaken for innocence. The Gazette has requested comment from the squirrels. None has been forthcoming.

Observation log • 4:17 p.m.

Squirrel Seen Transporting Unidentified Document

A resident reported a squirrel crossing the garden with what appeared to be a folded paper. No evidence has established that the paper was municipal property, but the witness considered the manner of transport “official-looking.”

Observation log • 11:36 a.m.

Bakery Visitor Departs Without Purchasing Anything

Witnesses confirmed that an unidentified individual entered a local bakery, looked around, and left. No laws are believed to have been broken. Neighbors have nevertheless requested that the Gazette keep the matter on file.

Neighborhood inquiry • Ongoing

Gazebo Activity Continues to Raise Questions

Residents have asked who authorized recent gazebo-related decisions, why the answers remain unsatisfactory, and whether the gazebo itself has been consulted. The Gazette has opened a file but has not determined what the file is for.

Witness statement • Evening hours

Suspiciously Prompt Curtain Closure Reported

A homeowner closed their curtains shortly after noticing a neighbor looking in their direction. The neighbor characterized the timing as “deliberate.” The homeowner declined to respond, citing the curtains.

The Gazette accepts eyewitness reports, speculation, and complaints of disproportionate emotional intensity. Allegations are published for community awareness and should not be confused with findings of fact.
