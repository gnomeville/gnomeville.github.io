OFFICIAL STATEMENT • OFFICE OF THE MAYOR

Mayor Thimblewick Addresses Recent Municipal Developments

October 26, 2026 | Gnomeville, Illinois

GNOMEVILLE, IL — In response to mounting public concern regarding a number of recent incidents, Mayor Thimblewick has issued the following statement. The Mayor's Office declined to specify which incidents prompted the announcement.

STATEMENT OF HIS HONOR, MAYOR THIMBLEWICK

My fellow residents, constituents, neighbors, and those who may have wandered into our municipality without realizing it:

Let me begin by saying, unequivocally, that Gnomeville is resilient, united, and absolutely not panicking.

Despite appearances.

I am aware that certain questions have arisen in recent days. Some of these questions are reasonable. Others are premature. Still others have been submitted in triplicate, which I find both encouraging and deeply troubling.

I wish to assure the public that my administration is taking these matters with the utmost seriousness.

Indeed, we have been taking them seriously for some time.

In certain instances, we began taking them seriously before we were aware of them.

That is the kind of proactive leadership the citizens of Gnomeville have come to expect.

There have been reports of disturbances, irregularities, unauthorized activities, unexplained developments, and what one particularly excitable individual described as “a complete breakdown of municipal civilization.”

I reject this characterization.

Gnomeville's civilization remains substantially intact.

Our roads continue to lead to their customary destinations, with certain exceptions. Our public institutions remain operational during those hours in which they are open. And our municipal records, I am assured, remain safely in the possession of whichever individuals currently possess them.

These are not the hallmarks of a community in crisis.

These are the hallmarks of a community experiencing events.

Regarding allegations of misconduct, I must emphasize that allegations are not findings, findings are not conclusions, and conclusions should not be reached before the appropriate committee has determined whether a committee is appropriate.

To that end, I have instructed the Municipal Committee for the Assessment of Potential Committee Formation to convene at its earliest convenience.

Its preliminary findings are expected shortly after its initial meeting, the scheduling of which remains under review.

I am particularly concerned by suggestions that certain members of our community have been unfairly scrutinized, improperly investigated, or subjected to unnecessary administrative procedures.

Let me be perfectly clear.

No resident of Gnomeville should ever be subjected to an unnecessary administrative procedure unless that procedure has been properly authorized.

This administration remains committed to transparency.

However, transparency must be balanced against confidentiality, discretion, operational security, personal privacy, municipal privilege, and the possibility that certain documents may prove embarrassing if examined too closely.

We therefore intend to be as transparent as circumstances permit, subject to the determination of those circumstances by the appropriate authorities.

I also wish to address the persistent rumors that my office has been slow to respond to recent developments.

Nothing could be further from the truth.

My office has responded promptly to every matter brought to my attention that I have deemed appropriate to acknowledge.

Matters not yet acknowledged remain under consideration.

Matters previously acknowledged may be reconsidered.

Matters requiring no acknowledgment will receive none.

This is not indecision.

This is governance.

Now, some have asked whether I intend to accept responsibility for the current situation.

I welcome that question.

Responsibility is the cornerstone of public service. It is a solemn obligation, a sacred trust, and a burden that no elected official should hesitate to discuss at considerable length.

And I assure you, when the time comes for responsibility to be assigned, my administration will ensure that it is assigned appropriately.

To the appropriate parties.

Through the appropriate channels.

At an appropriate time.

I hope that settles the matter.

Finally, I would remind every resident that Gnomeville has endured far greater challenges than those presently facing us, whatever those challenges may ultimately be determined to have been.

We have survived seasonal upheaval, infrastructure disputes, administrative misunderstandings, squirrel-related hostilities, and the regrettable incident involving the gazebo.

We shall survive this as well.

I ask only that you remain calm, exercise patience, respect your neighbors, and refrain from spreading unverified rumors unless they have first been published by a reputable local newspaper.

Above all, remember:

There is no cause for alarm.

And should cause for alarm subsequently be established, my office will issue a further statement explaining why the present statement should not be interpreted as having ruled it out.

Thank you.

God bless Gnomeville.

And please, for the love of all that is municipal, stop calling my office about the flamingos.

His Honor, Mayor Thimblewick
Office of the Mayor
Municipality of Gnomeville, Illinois

FOLLOWING THE STATEMENT

The Mayor declined to take questions, explaining that he had already answered them in principle.

When asked whether the administration intended to announce any concrete measures, a spokesperson replied that several measures were currently being measured.

The Mayor was subsequently observed returning to his office, where a glass of red wine had reportedly been waiting throughout the proceedings.

His office declined to confirm whether the glass was his.

The Gnomeville Gazette will continue to monitor the situation, assuming one exists.
