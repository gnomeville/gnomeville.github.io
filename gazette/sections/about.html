ABOUT THE GAZETTE

Independently Questionable Since 1969

All the News That's Fit to Fabricate.

ESTABLISHED 1969 YEARS OF CONTINUOUS PUBLICATION: DISPUTED EDITORIAL INTEGRITY: UNDER PERIODIC REVIEW

The Gnomeville Gazette is the municipality's leading source of news, information, public notices, and allegations presented with sufficient confidence to resemble facts.

Founded in 1969 under circumstances that remain poorly documented, the Gazette has proudly served generations of Gnomeville residents through periods of prosperity, uncertainty, municipal restructuring, and incidents involving the gazebo.

We remain committed to providing comprehensive coverage of the people, places, and events that shape our community.

Particularly when those people would prefer we didn't.

OUR MISSION

The Gnomeville Gazette is dedicated to the highest standards of community journalism.

We believe the public has a right to know what is happening in Gnomeville, why it is happening, who is responsible, and whether the Mayor intends to comment.

Our editorial principles are straightforward:

Accuracy: We make every reasonable effort to verify information before publication. Unreasonable efforts are evaluated individually.

Independence: The Gazette operates independently of municipal authorities, commercial interests, and personal grievances, except where existing arrangements make this impractical.

Accountability: We believe public officials should answer questions. Whether they do so is beyond our control.

Transparency: We are committed to explaining our editorial decisions whenever doing so would not create additional difficulties.

Community: We celebrate the achievements of our neighbors and report their shortcomings with equal enthusiasm.

OUR HISTORY

The Gazette published its first edition in 1969.

Unfortunately, the original printing records were subsequently misplaced during a municipal reorganization.

The circumstances surrounding this reorganization remain the subject of an ongoing archival inquiry.

Despite occasional interruptions, administrative confusion, and the disappearance of certain editorial personnel, the newspaper has continued publication.

Several issues have even appeared on schedule.

Today, the Gazette maintains a growing digital presence while preserving the traditions that made it an indispensable part of Gnomeville life.

Those traditions include investigative reporting, community announcements, unsolicited commentary, and refusing to explain the gazebo incident.

EDITORIAL INDEPENDENCE

The Gazette is not owned, operated, or controlled by the Municipality of Gnomeville.

Our editorial decisions are made without interference from the Mayor's Office, the Municipal Council, or any other governmental body.

The Gazette acknowledges that municipal officials occasionally express strong opinions regarding our coverage.

We welcome their feedback.

We are not required to enjoy it.

The Mayor's Office has requested that the preceding paragraph be removed.
The request is under consideration.

CORRECTIONS & CLARIFICATIONS

The Gnomeville Gazette takes its responsibility to readers seriously.

When factual errors are brought to our attention, we investigate promptly and determine whether a correction is warranted.

Corrections may be published in a subsequent edition, appended to the original article, or referred to the appropriate committee.

Readers requesting corrections should provide the article title, date of publication, nature of the alleged error, and supporting documentation.

Please note: Disagreement with an article does not constitute evidence of inaccuracy.

Neither does being the subject of the article.

CONTACT THE GAZETTE

We welcome news tips, letters, community announcements, photographs, advertising inquiries, and credible reports of municipal wrongdoing.

Unsubstantiated rumors will be evaluated according to their potential newsworthiness.

Submit a Story: Visit our Submit a Story section.

Letters to the Editor: Visit our Opinion section.

Complaints: Please submit your concerns in writing. Complaints regarding the complaints process must be submitted separately.

A FINAL WORD FROM THE EDITOR

For more than half a century, the Gnomeville Gazette has stood as a steadfast observer of municipal life.

We have reported on triumphs, tragedies, scandals, celebrations, unexplained disappearances, and the occasional squirrel.

We have asked difficult questions.

We have published uncomfortable truths.

We have also published several things that turned out not to be true, but were extremely interesting at the time.

Through it all, our commitment to Gnomeville has never wavered.

We are your newspaper.

We are your neighbors.

And we know what happened at the café last Thursday.
