How to Apply: A Simple Seven-Step Process

Streamlined following recommendations from the Municipal Committee for Administrative Simplification.

Determine Eligibility

Prove you are a resident, business, organization, or legally recognized entity. Applicants whose existence cannot be established may request provisional recognition, which does not establish existence.

Obtain Form ADV-001

Application for Permission to Request Advertising Space. This form permits you to request the forms necessary to apply. It is not itself an advertising application.

Submit Form ADV-002

Declare what you intend to advertise, whether it exists, whether it has ever existed, and whether you anticipate becoming the subject of an investigation. Uncertainty requires an interview.

Obtain Municipal Clearance

The Advertising Review Subcommittee meets on the third Thursday of each month, unless that Thursday conflicts with another Thursday. Acknowledgment of receipt does not indicate receipt.

Submit Advertising Materials

Claims must be true, plausibly true, or difficult to disprove. Known curses and pending litigation must be disclosed. Materials may be edited for comedic potential.

Remit Payment

Acorns, vouchers, approved barter, and certain precious metals accepted. Promises and Captain Peanuts' assurances are not. Form ADV-004 waives all expectations. Overpayments become donations.

Await Publication

Publication does not guarantee placement. Placement does not guarantee visibility. Applicants receiving no notification should not assume approval, rejection, or competence.

Appeals & Complaints

Submit Form ADV-009 within five business days of the decision. If no decision has been issued, request one using ADV-008. ADV-008 is available only to applicants who have received a decision. Procedural difficulties are not grounds for appeal.
