The Great Hall

Once the heart of an imposing stronghold, the Great Hall is now the heart of its hospitality.

Gatherings, stories and warmth

A closer look

A protected hearth glows beside comfortable sofas. Books and blankets are always within reach. Milo has his own little bed, because even the most formidable fortress must accommodate a cat.

An ancient commander's portrait watches over the proceedings, wearing a crooked paper party hat. Nobody remembers who put it there. Nobody has dared remove it.

Hall protocol

Guests are welcome to sit, chat, read, and share snacks. A quiet afternoon is considered a successful military operation.

The commander has survived three centuries. The party hat is staying.
