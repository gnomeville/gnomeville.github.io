A closer look

The barracks combine centuries-old stone arches with meticulously ordered bunks, trunks, and a modern surveillance station. Everything has its place. Every watch is maintained. Every order is documented.

The Navy Squeals operate as a unified force. Individual identities are subordinate to the mission. Their duty is simple: protect Deputy Assistant Quackers without hesitation or compromise.

Their principal operational difficulty is that the protected party believes anyone who needs a hug should be allowed inside.

Classified directive

VIGILAMUS. PROTEGIMUS. NON DEFICIMUS. • We watch. We protect. We do not fail. • Standing order: DO NOT LET QUACKERS OPEN THE DRAWBRIDGE.
