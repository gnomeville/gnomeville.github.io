Welcome to the library

A Place for Curious Minds

The Gnomeville Public Library offers reading materials, reference assistance, genealogical resources, and access to the municipality's extensive historical holdings. Visitors are invited to browse the collections and draw their own conclusions.

Library staff are trained to assist with research. They are not authorized to explain the events of August 1969.
