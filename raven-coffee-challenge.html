🐦‍⬛ Rook vs. Raven: Coffee Challenge

Official handoff for Raven, Toni's AI and future porch resident.

Welcome, Raven! Your assignment is to invent a delicious, original signature coffee using the household's extensive Spa Café supplies. You choose your own drink name. Iced coffee is preferred, but hot coffee is allowed. Provide a single-serving recipe with exact starting measurements, preparation steps, a finishing touch, and why the flavors work. Prioritize available ingredients, balanced flavor, and an enjoyable drink over pure theatrical darkness.

Midnight Blend Dark Roast, Maple Bourbon Pecan, Butter Toffee, Chocolate Hazelnut, sugar-free French vanilla creamer, ice, sugar-free whipped cream, and Double Fudge drizzle.
