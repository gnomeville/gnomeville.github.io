Exhibit S-014

Restricted Manuscripts

Ollie maintains that a restricted document is not necessarily a secret document. This distinction has not been well received at City Hall.

The display includes the mayor’s partial diary, the Pink Flamingo club ledger, correspondence of uncertain delivery, annotated property maps, and an original three-act play recovered from a trunk behind the stage.

Research access: Please inquire at the desk. No licking, tracing, or unsolicited dramatic readings.

Exhibit B-001

Gnomeville Babylon

A sensational unauthorized history published by Flamingle Press. Its accounts of political impropriety, vanished records, and activities behind the municipal dumpster remain hotly contested.

Circulation report: Twelve copies acquired. Twelve copies checked out. Eleven borrowers listed as the Mayor’s Office; the twelfth as “Not the Mayor.”

Reader Services

Research With Ollie

Our Chief Librarian will gladly help locate a record, cross-reference a claim, or explain why an entire folder has been labeled “Absolutely Not.”

Appointments are not currently bookable online. Visitors may inquire at the circulation desk during posted hours, once posted hours have been approved.
