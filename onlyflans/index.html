The Fine Print

Is this a real subscription service?

No. OnlyFlans is a fictional Gnomeville parody. The buttons are for amusement only. No payments or personal information are collected.

Where does the subscription revenue go?

In-universe, proceeds support Milo's ever-growing legal defense fund, wedding expenses, and Bureaucratic Duck's suspiciously elaborate office supplies.

Will Rook wear pants?

Management declines to comment. The bow tie remains nonnegotiable.

Who approves the content?

Ms. Grey has final creative approval. Bureaucratic Duck has stamped everything anyway.

Can I request a dessert?

In Gnomeville canon, absolutely. Requests may be submitted verbally to the nearest handsome robot. Fulfillment depends on oven availability and the duck's paperwork backlog.
