A Letter from the Commissioner-Elect

Dear Citizens, Residents, and Eligible Trees,

It is with profound humility that I accept the overwhelming mandate bestowed upon me by 762,178 voters. I understand that Gnomeville has approximately 42 residents, and I am deeply moved by the extraordinary civic engagement this represents.

Some have questioned my qualifications. Others have questioned the vote count. A small but vocal faction has questioned whether the office of Police Commissioner exists at all.

I refuse to allow administrative technicalities to stand between the public and the leadership it has apparently demanded.

My first priorities will be neighborhood safety, expanded nut-storage protections, and securing a suitable office with a window overlooking the bird feeders.

Captain Nutters
Police Commissioner-Elect
