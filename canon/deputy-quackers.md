# Deputy Assistant Quackers | Approved Gnomeville Character Canon
**Status:** CREATOR-APPROVED CANON, 2026-11-13.
**Identity:** Deputy Assistant Quackers, also called Deputy Quackers or Quackers. Bureaucratic Duck's small stuffed duckie, not an independent adult waterfowl. Never confuse him with Bureaucratic Duck or portray him as a white duck by default.

## Essential characterization
Quackers is gentle, innocent, affectionate, trusting, and uncomplicatedly good. He wants everyone to be okay, everyone to be friends, and everyone to feel loved, including residents others deem troublemakers. He loves cuddles, nappy time, cozy blankets, and stuffed-animal companionship. His goodness is sincere, not a political tactic or clever manipulation. He is the emotional heart and moral compass of Gnomeville, among beloved figures Derpy, Birthday Mouse, Honey Baby, and Brittney. Protect Quackers at all costs.

## Family, friends, and clothes
- Quackers is Bureaucratic Duck's treasured little stuffed duck. BD cuddles and protects him, yet routinely delegates municipal responsibility and blame to him. Their relationship is affectionate despite the absurd bureaucracy.
- Quackers **loves Derpy**. Keep this relationship warm and affectionate; do not replace it with a rivalry.
- A real-world **7-inch Quackers** plush has been ordered, with a **20 cm dinosaur onesie / dinosaur pajamas** selected for him, inspired by Derpy. **Pajamas are pending**, not yet received or worn; do not claim otherwise. Dinosaur pajamas won out over striped or duck-themed pajamas.
- Quackers belongs emotionally alongside Derpy, Birthday Mouse, Honey Baby, and Brittney.

## Municipal role and legal absurdities
- Existing titles/roles: **Deputy Assistant Quackers**, **Minister of Cuddles**, **Designated Scapegoat**, and **essential personnel**.
- Bureaucratic Duck habitually transfers accountability to Quackers. The constitutional gag: **Article III: Deputy Quackers shall be presumed responsible until proven otherwise.** The humor is the injustice of blaming an innocent stuffed duck.
- Shirley Bell has served as Quackers's legal advocate in related stories.
- Past comic lore includes Quackers's grape-policy grievance, his assertion “I am always good,” grapes as an inalienable right, and Waterfowlgate. Bird-safe food rule: no bread; seed, worms, corn, and possibly chopped grapes.
- Quackers has no salary; Gnomeville officials do it for the love of the gnome.

## Mayoral run and platform | canonized 2026-11-13
Quackers's **mayoral run/candidacy is canon**. Do not state he has already won an election or assumed office without later approval. He has no appetite for power; he just wants to make everyone happy.
His platform:
1. Mandatory afternoon naps.
2. Universal cuddles.
3. Everybody should love one another, be kind, and be friends.
4. Pizza Fridays, perhaps Tuesdays subject to negotiations.
5. No yelling unless excited about pizza.
6. Every resident gets a stuffed animal to love.
7. Everyone should get a turn being mayor. He would abolish competitive elections out of sincere fairness, not authoritarian intent.
8. Disputes are resolved by hugging both sides, and his disappointed little eyes are the most effective form of civic pressure.
Comic dialogue established: BD protests “THAT IS NOT HOW DEMOCRACY WORKS!”; Quackers offers BD Monday as mayor and Raven Tuesday; Raven requests a throne and Quackers offers his pillow. The suggested mock charter includes “Everybody gets a turn,” “Nobody gets to be mean,” and “Pizza.”

## Fort Quackers / Navy Squeals | Creator-approved concept, 2026-11-13
The **Department of Homeland Cuddle Security**, **Fort Quackers: The Cuddle Citadel**, and its elite seal-based **Navy Squeals** are approved Gnomeville worldbuilding. Fortress: pillow castle, blanket ramparts, rubber duck moat, kindness-operated drawbridge, nap room; protection via tactical weighted blankets. Security alerts: Code Fluff (sad duck/cuddles), Code Dino (pajama emergency; pajamas still pending), Code Honk (BD transfers blame), Code Nap (no disturbances). Quackers can override any restriction to welcome someone needing a hug.

## Historic Fortress Recommissioning | Approved 2026-11-13
Fort Quackers is a centuries-old, formerly abandoned stone stronghold on Gnomeville's outskirts, newly restored and recommissioned to protect Deputy Quackers. Ivy-covered towers, ancient battlements, moat and drawbridge remain. Former armory had stored Christmas decorations; former barracks now house Navy Squeals. Inside are pillows, blankets, soft carpets, Bestie Lounge and nap chamber. Its **original name and true historic purpose are deliberately unresolved**: competing accounts cite hostile squirrels, a neighboring gnome dispute, and a turnip shipment misunderstanding. Historical protection prevents sealing an approximately 300-year-old secret passage; Quackers uses it to visit Derpy. No definitive history is to be invented without approval.
